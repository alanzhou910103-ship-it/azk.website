Samples, Branding & Packaging: What to Confirm

A useful first quotation starts with the exact model and the type of sample you need. Evaluation samples, unbranded samples and custom-branded production are different requests.

Ordering & Shipping FAQ

Small wholesale orders, custom branding and dropshipping are different services. Check the options below before requesting a quotation.

Can I start with 5 or 10 units?

Yes. Small wholesale orders of 5 or 10 units are welcome. These orders use the factory’s existing logo and factory-branded packaging, without custom branding. Confirm the model and current availability when requesting a quote.

Can you ship each order directly to my U.S. customers?

We do not currently offer dropshipping to individual U.S. customers. For small wholesale purchases, you arrange fulfillment to your customers. We may consider U.S. dropshipping if we establish a local warehouse, but there is no confirmed timeline.

Can my Shopify store automatically send orders to you for fulfillment?

We do not currently provide a Shopify integration for automated dropshipping fulfillment. We would evaluate this alongside future U.S. warehousing; it is not a service available today.

Can I use my own brand on the products and packaging?

For G1, G2, G6 Pro and G7, silk-screen logo printing on standard black or white controllers requires 500 units per model, per color. Black and white quantities cannot be combined: 250 black plus 250 white does not meet the logo-printing minimum. Custom color boxes and manuals are available from 500 units per model. Custom shell colors require at least 1,000 units per color. Confirm artwork, specifications and costs before production.

Is neutral packaging available for small orders?

Small wholesale orders come with the factory’s existing branded packaging. Neutral packaging and your-brand packaging are not the standard options for 5- or 10-unit orders. Unbranded evaluation samples for selected models are a separate arrangement; see the sample details below.

What are the shipping costs and delivery times to the United States?

For wholesale deliveries to your receiving address, sea freight can be a lower-cost option, while express shipping generally costs more. We can check suitable services, including 4PX where applicable. Send the model, quantity and delivery ZIP code for a quotation with estimated transit time. Rates and timing depend on the shipment and route; there is no fixed website-wide rate.

How do I request 1–2 evaluation samples?

Please include:

  • Model and preferred color.
  • Sample quantity: 1–2 units.
  • Recipient name and contact details.
  • Destination country, full delivery address and postal code. The address and postal code are essential for calculating international shipping costs.

If you use a freight forwarder in mainland China, we can send the samples to that forwarder. Provide the forwarder’s mainland China receiving address, contact details and any required shipment reference; your overseas delivery address is not needed for this domestic shipment.

Sample availability and order requirements depend on the model. Confirm the sample price, shipping cost and dispatch timing when requesting a quotation.

When will evaluation samples be dispatched?

Evaluation samples of G1, G2, G6 Pro and G7 are usually dispatched within 2 days after payment. This is the time before shipment, not the delivery time to your address. Confirm availability and the shipping arrangement with your quotation.

B2B returns of non-defective goods

For business purchases for resale or commercial use, we do not accept change-of-mind returns of non-defective goods. Slow sales, unsold stock, excess inventory or a change in purchasing plans are not grounds for a return. Cross-border returns involve complex import procedures, so please confirm the model, specifications and quantity before ordering.

Quality faults are handled through the warranty and replacement process described below. This B2B policy does not limit mandatory rights under applicable law.

Warranty and replacement support

G1, G2, G6 Pro and G7 have a 12-month warranty from the date you receive the products. Provide a clear video showing any quality fault for review. Once the quality issue is confirmed, the faulty product does not need to be returned. We cover the cost of replacement and its shipping, and send the replacement together with your next order. Replacements are not dispatched separately immediately. This process applies to evaluation samples and bulk orders.

Which samples can be unbranded?

Unbranded samples can currently be requested for accessories, G17, G9 and G3, subject to the selected configuration and current availability. Evaluation samples of other controller models normally carry the existing brand logo. For a bulk project, ask us about an unbranded or your-brand version of the chosen controller.

If the absence of a logo is essential to your evaluation, state that in the first message. We can then confirm whether the selected model and sample configuration match your requirement before you pay for a sample.

When can a logo or custom packaging be added?

Standard evaluation samples cannot be custom silk-screen printed or laser engraved. For a custom bulk order, we prepare logo and color-box samples for your approval before production, usually taking around 7 days. A physical manual sample is not normally prepared, but can be arranged if requested. This pre-production approval stage is separate from the usual 2-day dispatch of standard evaluation samples. Silk-screen printing and laser engraving for bulk orders depend on the model, marking position and order details.

For graphic projects such as the GA06L charging grip, artwork, process and final finish need sample approval. The proposed artwork shown on a page is not automatically an approved production specification.

Custom sampling and setup fees

  • Basic silk-screen logo and standard color-box proofs: no additional sampling fee.
  • Waterslide decals or water-transfer printing: a plate-making / artwork setup fee applies.
  • Premium custom color boxes: an additional sampling fee may apply.

The exact charge depends on the artwork, decoration process and packaging requirements and is confirmed in the project quotation. These customization proofing fees are separate from the paid evaluation samples described above.

What about CE, RoHS and FCC documents?

For G1, G2, G6 Pro and G7, we can provide English manuals, high-resolution product images and CE, RoHS and FCC documents. You may use the supplied manuals and product images on your own website. Certification documents must be checked against the exact model, sales version and destination requirements before use. Battery transport documents, including UN38.3 and MSDS, are also available. Request the documents for the exact battery and product version so the shipping arrangement can be checked.

Inspection and consistency with approved samples

You may appoint a third-party inspector to visit the factory before shipment. If you cannot arrange inspection, we can provide videos of the actual batch, function tests and packaging photos.

Bulk production follows the approved sample configuration. If a chip shortage requires a chip change, we will notify you in advance.

Firmware updates

Firmware upgrades are normally performed using a computer. We provide the update installation package for the relevant model. Contact us with your model and the issue you are experiencing for the appropriate update guidance.

Location and order documents

INT-ROCK is a trading business based in Shenzhen, China, working with factories in mainland China. We can provide a pro forma invoice (PI) and commercial invoice (CI) for your order.

Sample fees, payment and production timing

  • Samples: paid in full before dispatch. The sample fee can be credited against a subsequent bulk order.
  • Bulk payment: 30% deposit; the remaining 70% is payable after inspection and before shipment.
  • Custom production: usually 30–35 days after we receive the deposit and you approve the sample and artwork. This is production time, not shipping time. Confirm timing for existing-brand orders with your quotation.

International shipping and tax-inclusive delivery

We can arrange international express, air freight and sea freight, or deliver to your freight forwarder in mainland China. Tax-inclusive door-to-door quotations are available depending on the destination and shipment. Send the delivery address and postal code for a shipment-specific quotation; freight is quoted case by case.

Send a brief we can quote accurately

Include these details in your inquiry:

  1. Model and the features or platform you need. Use our controller selection guide if you are still comparing.
  2. Sample type: evaluation sample with the existing brand, or an unbranded sample where available.
  3. Estimated production quantity and whether you need your own logo, color, artwork or packaging.
  4. Destination country, sales channel and any documentation requirements.
  5. Target delivery date and shipping destination.

We will confirm sample availability, minimum order quantity, pricing and timing for that exact brief. Send your requirements or email sales@int-rock.com.
