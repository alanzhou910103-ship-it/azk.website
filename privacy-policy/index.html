Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 13, 2026

int-rock (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website int-rock.com or contact us regarding our products and services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, company name, and phone number when you submit a contact form or request a quote.
  • Communication Records: Contents of messages you send to us via email or contact forms.
  • Usage Data: We use Google Analytics 4 to measure page visits, product views, contact-link clicks and successfully submitted inquiries. Google Analytics may process device, browser, approximate location and cookie-based identifiers. We do not send inquiry names, email addresses, company names or message text as analytics event parameters.
  • Inquiry Sources: Contact forms can include campaign labels, the landing-page path and the referring website origin so we can understand which channels bring inquiries. These source details may be retained in browser session storage during your visit.

We do not collect payment information or sensitive personal data through this website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Process wholesale and partnership requests
  • Improve our website and user experience
  • Send relevant product and business updates (only if you opt in)

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with trusted service providers who assist in website analytics or email delivery, subject to confidentiality agreements.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not function properly.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., social media platforms). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at sales@int-rock.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy:

Email: sales@int-rock.com
