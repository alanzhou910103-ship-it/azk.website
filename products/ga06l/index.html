Charging Grip with Back Controls

The GA06L charging grip is designed for Joy-Con 2 controllers. It combines three interchangeable back button sets with USB-C power input for charging during supported tabletop and TV-mode use.

Molded rails hold the controllers in position, while the back controls can be mapped through compatible console settings. Joy-Con 2 controllers and an external power source are not included unless specified in the order.

Three Back Button Styles

The GA06L includes three interchangeable GL/GR back button styles: trigger-style, paddle-style, and direct-press. Buyers can select the shape that best suits their target customer and product positioning.

The button caps can also carry coordinated graphics for branded editions. Mapping behavior depends on the console settings and selected product configuration.

Charge While You Play

The USB-C input supplies external power for charging the installed Joy-Con 2 controllers during supported use. An external power source and cable are required.

The charging grip is intended for tabletop and TV-mode play. Charging behavior should be confirmed with the final sample and intended power source.

Tool-Free Customization

The center panel and back button sets can be changed without tools. This supports different visual treatments, button shapes, and more compact storage.

The contoured, textured shell is designed to provide a secure hold during regular play. Confirm assembly, finish, and button feel during sample approval.

Buyer Questions

Does GA06L charge without a cable?

No. The USB-C input requires wired external power. The charging grip is not a power bank.

Can it be used while Joy-Con 2 controllers are attached to the console?

The charging grip holds detached Joy-Con 2 controllers for tabletop or TV-mode play. It is not a handheld charging attachment for the console.

What should I check in a customized sample?

Check artwork placement and color, surface finish, controller fit, charging with the intended power source, and GL/GR button operation in compatible console settings.
