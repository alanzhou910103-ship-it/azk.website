Upgrade Your Gaming with Pro-Level Control

The GA06L charging grip is designed for Switch 2 Joy-Con controllers, featuring 3 interchangeable back paddle sets and pass-through charging so you never have to pause the action.

Precision-molded rails hold Joy-Cons firmly in place with zero wobble, while responsive trigger pass-through ensures no input lag during competitive play.

3 Back Paddle Sets for Every Playstyle

Why settle for one back button layout? The GA06L ships with three complete paddle sets — short, long, and hybrid — so you can switch configurations to match whatever game you're playing. FPS? Go with short triggers. Racing? Use long paddles for precise throttle control.

Mix and match different paddle types on the same grip. Unlike competing products that lock you into one configuration, the GA06L gives you complete freedom over your control layout.

Charge While You Play

The built-in USB-C pass-through charging port means your Joy-Cons stay powered while you game. No interruptions, no swapping controllers mid-match, no battery anxiety. Just plug in and keep playing.

Works in TV mode and tabletop mode — the charging grip becomes your command center for marathon gaming sessions.

Tool-Free Customization

Everything on the GA06L snaps on and off without tools. Swap back paddle sets in seconds. Remove the grip handle for compact storage when traveling. Reconfigure between games without touching a single screw.

The textured, ergonomic surface provides a secure non-slip hold even during the most intense moments, while the contoured shape significantly reduces hand fatigue over long play sessions.
