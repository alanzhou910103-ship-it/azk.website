Feature Overview

Hall Effect Joysticks

Contactless magnetic sensing supports smooth, precise control and improved resistance to joystick drift.

Adjustable Turbo

Manual and automatic Turbo modes support three selectable speeds: 8, 12, or 20 inputs per second.

Four Programmable Back Buttons

M1-M4 rear buttons support macros with up to 16 inputs for flexible control in compatible games.

Customizable RGB Lighting

Multiple joystick-lighting modes, eight selectable colors, and four brightness levels personalize the setup.

Motion & Adjustable Vibration

Six-axis motion control and four vibration levels add responsive control and feedback in compatible games.

Integrated Game Audio

Built-in speaker, microphone, microphone control, and a 3.5 mm headset jack support game audio and voice chat.

Hall Effect Control with Adjustable Turbo

Hall Effect joysticks use contactless magnetic sensing to provide smooth, precise input and improved resistance to joystick drift. The ergonomic shell and textured grips are designed for comfortable control during longer sessions.

Turbo can be assigned to the face buttons and L1, L2, R1, and R2. Manual and automatic modes are available, with adjustable rates of 8, 12, or 20 inputs per second.

Programmable Back Controls

Four rear buttons - M1, M2, M3, and M4 - can be programmed for easier access to frequently used commands. Each macro supports a sequence of up to 16 inputs.

The included SET control can enable or disable the programmable-button function, helping prevent unintended inputs when back controls are not required.

Motion, Vibration and RGB Lighting

A high-precision six-axis gyroscope supports motion-based control in compatible games. Dual motors offer four vibration levels: Off, 30%, 70%, and 100%, with 70% selected by default.

RGB lighting around the joysticks includes multiple effects, eight selectable colors, and four brightness levels. The controller remembers the selected brightness after power-off.

Multi-Platform Wireless Play

G17 is designed for PS5, PS5 Slim, PS5 Pro, PC, iOS, and Android. Bluetooth 5.1 provides stable wireless connectivity, while USB-C supports wired connection and charging.

Initial pairing with a PS5 console requires the supplied USB cable. Once paired, the controller can reconnect wirelessly with the Home button. Functions may vary by platform, game, and connection mode.

Touchpad, Speaker and Headset Support

The controller includes a multi-touch touchpad, built-in speaker, built-in microphone, and a dedicated microphone control button.

A 3.5 mm stereo headset jack supports compatible headsets for game audio and microphone communication.
