Precision TMR Control

The G1 Dasheng 3 wireless game controller is built around TMR magnetic joysticks for accurate directional control and strong return-to-center performance. It is designed to help reduce drift during extended use.

Mechanical micro buttons, RGB lighting, dual macro back buttons, Turbo, and app-based button programming give players practical ways to tailor the controller to their preferred games.

Two Trigger Modes for Different Games

Switch to 8mm linear trigger travel when smooth modulation matters in racing games, or use the 0.8mm micro-trigger mode when a shorter press is preferred for FPS play.

The two trigger modes let one controller support different game genres without changing your setup.

Custom Controls, Your Way

The G1 includes interchangeable D-pad style options, dual macro back buttons, and mobile-app customization for joystick adjustment, button programming, Turbo, and vibration settings.

Six-axis motion sensing and dual rotor vibration motors with four adjustment levels add more control options across compatible games.

Tri-Mode, Multi-Platform Play

Choose wired, Bluetooth, or 2.4G connectivity to match your setup. The G1 supports Switch, Switch 2, PC, Android, and iOS devices.

An 800mAh rechargeable battery, Type-C charging port, and included 2.4G receiver make it ready for wireless play out of the box. Check game and platform requirements for supported functions.
