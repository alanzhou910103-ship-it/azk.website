A Fuller Handheld Grip

These snap-on grips are designed for Joy-Con 2 controllers. Their contoured shape adds more palm support for customers who find the standard handheld profile too slim.

The lightweight two-piece design keeps the controllers portable while providing a fuller grip during handheld play.

Access to the Main Controls

The molded openings are positioned around the buttons, triggers, shoulder controls, and thumbsticks. The low-profile edges are designed to preserve normal reach to the main controls.

Check the sample fit and control clearance with the intended Joy-Con 2 version before confirming a production order.

Dock-Compatible & Wrist-Strap Ready

The GA05 uses a slim profile designed to remain attached when the console is placed in a standard Switch 2 dock. Dock tolerances and third-party dock designs can vary, so fit should be confirmed during sampling.

The open-rail design keeps the wrist-strap connection accessible for compatible accessories and motion-control setups.

Snap On, Snap Off in Seconds

Installation is tool-free: align each grip with the corresponding Joy-Con 2 controller and press it into place. The grips can be removed for storage or transport.

Each grip weighs approximately 15g. The textured surface is intended to improve hold and reduce visible fingerprints during normal use.
