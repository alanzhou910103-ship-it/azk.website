Built for Nintendo Switch 2

The G8 is designed for Nintendo Switch 2 and includes a dedicated C Button and console wake-up support. Function availability can vary by firmware, game, and connection mode, so confirm the required setup before ordering.

A built-in 3.5mm headset jack supports compatible wired headsets when the active platform and connection mode provide audio.

Precision Meets Performance

The G8 uses Hall Effect joysticks with contactless magnetic sensing. This design is intended to provide stable centering and reduce wear-related drift compared with conventional potentiometer sticks.

Nine-axis motion sensing supports aiming, steering, and other motion-based controls in compatible games.

Tri-Mode Freedom

The G8 supports 2.4GHz wireless, Bluetooth 5.3, and USB-C wired connections. These options cover different use cases, from a receiver-based setup to direct Bluetooth pairing and wired play.

Platform support and available functions vary by connection mode. Confirm the required platform, receiver package, and audio functions before placing an order.

Gaming Marathon Ready

The 800mAh rechargeable battery provides up to approximately 15 hours of use under typical conditions. Actual runtime varies with vibration, connection mode, headset use, and other settings. Charging takes approximately two hours with a suitable USB-C power source.

Dual-motor vibration provides feedback in compatible games, while the contoured grip is designed for comfortable everyday use.
