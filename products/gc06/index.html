TMR Replacement Module for Compatible Controllers

GC06 is a third-party replacement joystick module designed for compatible PlayStation 5 DualSense Edge wireless controllers. It combines TMR sensing with an adjustable-tension mechanism in a compact, replaceable format.

The module is intended for customers who want a serviceable joystick option and a choice of stick resistance. Confirm controller compatibility before purchase or installation.

Tool-Free Slide-In Installation

The replacement process uses the controller's removable joystick-module access system. Open the compatible module cover, release the existing module, then slide the replacement module into place until it is seated securely.

Follow the controller manufacturer's instructions during installation. Avoid forcing the module or touching internal contacts, and test the controller after replacement.

Built for an Accessory and Service Lineup

The GC06 module is positioned for gaming-accessory distributors, repair-focused sellers, and customers looking for a compatible replacement module. Its compact form makes it suitable for standalone retail, repair bundles, and after-sales accessory programs.

PlayStation and DualSense Edge are trademarks of Sony Interactive Entertainment. GC06 is a third-party accessory and is not an official Sony product.
