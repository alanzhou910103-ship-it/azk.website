Flexible Wireless Control

The G3 combines 2.4GHz wireless, Bluetooth 5.2, and USB-C wired connectivity in one controller. Check the product specifications and your device setup for supported platforms and functions.

Ergonomic contours and textured grip surfaces are designed to support comfortable extended sessions.

Six Signature Colors

The G3 is available in six colorways: Midnight Black, Galaxy White, Deep Sea Blue, Sakura Pink, Mecha Green, and Steel Gray. This range supports different retail themes and customer preferences.

Confirm finish samples and final color standards before production, especially for customized orders.

Configurable Controls

Physical trigger stops allow the user to switch between standard and shorter trigger travel. Button remapping, onboard profiles, and included accessories can vary by product version and should be confirmed for the selected order.

Sample testing is recommended to verify the required controls, connection modes, and package contents before production.

Game Longer, Charge Less

The G3 uses a 1000mAh rechargeable battery and offers up to 20 hours of play time, depending on connection mode and usage. USB-C charging supports convenient recharging between sessions.

Use the supplied instructions for charging and power-management guidance for your specific configuration.
