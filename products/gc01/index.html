Lightweight Protection That Travels as Light as Your Console

Designed for the Switch 2 console, the GC01 combines a compact carrying profile with an EVA hard shell. The structure helps protect the console from everyday bumps and surface contact during transport.

The exterior uses 600D water-repellent Oxford fabric for added resistance to light splashes and daily wear. It is not intended for immersion or heavy rain.

Organized Storage for Gamers On the Go

Integrated elastic slots organize up to 12 game cards in one section of the case. This keeps the cards separated from loose items and easy to find.

The soft interior lining helps reduce direct contact with the screen and Joy-Con 2 controllers during transport.

Designed with Thumbstick Clearance

The GC01 includes a recessed area for compatible Joy-Con 2 thumb grips and provides clearance around the thumbsticks. Confirm fit with larger third-party grips during sampling.

A wide hook-and-loop strap helps keep the console in position while the case is being carried.

Smooth Access, Secure Carry

Dual zippers allow the case to open widely for access to the console and storage area.

A reinforced webbing hand strap supports hand carrying and can also be attached to a suitable carabiner. The compact profile is designed for backpacks, messenger bags, and carry-on luggage.
