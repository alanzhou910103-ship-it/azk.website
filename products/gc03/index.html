Protection Without the Bulk

The GC03 is a molded protective faceplate for the Nintendo Switch 2 console. It adds a slim protective layer while keeping the console's familiar shape.

The faceplate helps reduce daily scratches and scuffs while providing access to the controls, ports, and ventilation areas.

Dock Without Removing

The GC03 uses a slim profile designed to remain installed when the console is placed in a Switch 2 dock. Dock tolerances can vary, so fit should be confirmed with the selected dock during sampling.

Cutouts provide access to the USB-C charging port, headphone jack, and ventilation areas. Check the final sample to confirm clearance for the required cables and accessories.

Color Options for Different Markets

The GC03 is shown in Midnight Black, Ocean Blue, Steel Gray, Neon Orange, and Plum Purple. This selection supports different retail themes and customer preferences.

Confirm available colors, finish samples, and final color standards before production.

Control and Accessory Clearance

The GC03 is shaped to provide clearance around the thumbsticks, buttons, and triggers for normal control movement.

It can be used with selected thumb grips and button caps, but accessory dimensions vary. Confirm fit with the required accessories during sampling. Installation uses a tool-free snap-on design.
