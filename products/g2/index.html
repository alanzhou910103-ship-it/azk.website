Precision You Can Personalize

The G2 is a customizable wireless controller built around TMR magnetic joysticks, dual-mode triggers, RGB lighting, a magnetic replaceable faceplate, and a battery percentage display.

Two macro back buttons, six-axis gyroscope control, app-based button mapping, Turbo, vibration, RGB, and joystick adjustment give players practical ways to tune the controller for their setup.

Two Trigger Modes for Racing and Shooting

Use the 9mm linear trigger mode for smoother throttle control in racing games, or change to the 0.3mm micro-trigger mode when a shorter response is preferred for FPS games.

One controller can support different play styles without requiring a separate gaming setup.

Layouts and Controls That Adapt

A one-key switch changes between Xbox-style and NS button layouts for familiar controls on compatible PC and Switch setups. The Xbox-style layout does not indicate Xbox console compatibility.

Two joystick height options and interchangeable cross and disc D-pad styles help players choose the feel that suits their games.

Battery Status and Charging Options

A built-in percentage display keeps battery status visible at a glance. The G2 has an 800mAh lithium battery and a Type-C charging port.

The smart charging dock version supports drop-and-charge use. Please confirm the selected package configuration before placing a wholesale order.
