Wholesale & OEM Project Basics

What is the typical minimum order quantity?

MOQ depends on the model, branding method and packaging. Send your target quantity for a model-specific quotation.

How long does sample preparation take?

Sample availability, price and preparation time depend on the model and current stock. We confirm them in the sample quotation.

What is the typical production lead time?

Lead time depends on materials, order size and customization. We confirm the schedule after the product and order details are agreed.

Can you support compliance documentation?

CE, RoHS and FCC documentation is available for many models. Ask us to confirm the exact report for your selected model, version and destination market.

Which products have unbranded samples?

Unbranded samples are currently available for accessories, G17, G9 and G3. Other controller samples carry the existing brand; unbranded or your-brand production can be discussed for bulk orders.

Can samples be printed with my logo?

Samples are not available with custom silk-screen printing or laser engraving. These marking options can be discussed for bulk production. Standard products use color-box packaging; custom packaging depends on order volume.
