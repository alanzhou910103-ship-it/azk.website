One Kit for Multiple Play Modes

The GA06P is a multi-part accessory kit for Joy-Con 2 controllers. It combines a USB-C charging grip, three interchangeable back button sets, and a pair of detachable ergonomic grips.

The package gives retailers one product that covers handheld grip support plus tabletop and TV-mode charging-grip use.

Charging Grip + Back Buttons

The included charging grip holds both Joy-Con 2 controllers while a USB-C input supplies external power for charging during supported tabletop and TV-mode use. An external power source is required.

Three GL/GR back button sets are included: trigger-style, paddle-style, and direct-press. They can be changed without tools, and mapping behavior depends on compatible console settings.

Detachable Joy-Con 2 Grips

The included snap-on handles add a contoured palm area to each individual Joy-Con 2 controller. They are intended for customers who prefer a fuller grip during handheld play.

The grips attach without tools and can be removed for storage, transport, or use with the charging grip.

Use Across Play Modes

Use the charging grip with detached Joy-Con 2 controllers for tabletop or TV-mode play, and use the individual grips while the controllers are attached for handheld play.

The textured surfaces and contoured shapes are designed to provide a more secure hold. Confirm fit, finish, charging behavior, and button mapping during sample approval.

Buyer questions

What is included in the kit?

One charging grip, three interchangeable GL/GR back-button sets, one pair of individual Joy-Con 2 grips and one USB-C cable. Confirm final package contents in the order specification; Joy-Con 2 controllers are not included.

How are the parts used across play modes?

Use the individual grips for handheld play and the charging grip with detached Joy-Con 2 controllers for tabletop or TV-mode play.

Does the charging grip contain a backup battery?

The charging grip requires wired external power through USB-C; it is not a power bank.
