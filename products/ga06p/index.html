Complete Your Pro Setup

The GA06P is the all-in-one bundle designed for Switch 2 Joy-Con controllers. It combines a pass-through charging grip, 3 interchangeable back paddle sets, and a pair of detachable ergonomic grips — everything you need for pro-level control and comfort.

From competitive multiplayer to immersive single-player adventures, the GA06P gives you the tools to play your best.

Charging Grip + Back Paddles

The included charging grip holds both Joy-Cons securely while charging via USB-C. Play in TV or tabletop mode without worrying about battery life. Precision-molded rails ensure zero wobble and responsive trigger pass-through with no input lag.

Three complete back paddle sets — short, long, and hybrid — let you customize your controls for any game. Swap them in seconds without tools. Mix and match different styles on the same grip for ultimate flexibility.

Detachable Joy-Con Grips

The included snap-on handles transform each individual Joy-Con with a contoured, full-palm grip. Handheld mode finally feels like a pro controller — no more cramped hands during long gaming sessions.

The grips are fully detachable and tool-free, attaching in seconds when you need them and popping off just as easily for travel or docked play.

Play Your Way, Any Mode

The GA06P works seamlessly across all Switch 2 play modes. Use the charging grip for TV and tabletop gaming. Snap on the individual grips for comfortable handheld sessions. Everything is interchangeable and compatible.

The textured, ergonomic surfaces on every component provide a secure non-slip hold, while the contoured shapes reduce fatigue during marathon gaming sessions.
