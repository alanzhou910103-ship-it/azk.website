Hall Effect Control

The G11 uses Hall Effect magnetic sensing in the joysticks and triggers. The contactless design is intended to provide stable centering and reduce wear-related drift over long-term use.

Adjustable trigger stops let the user choose between longer analog travel and a shorter trigger response for different game types.

Built for Competitive Play

Physical trigger-stop switches and four removable rear controls give players practical ways to adjust the controller for different game types. The 2.4GHz wireless mode is intended for responsive receiver-based play on supported devices.

Mechanical face buttons provide a defined click feel, while the textured grips support a secure hold during longer sessions.

Customizable to Your Playstyle

The G11 supports interchangeable thumbstick options, adjustable trigger travel, programmable rear controls, and vibration adjustment. Available settings and profile behavior depend on the selected product version.

Designed for Everyday Durability

The controller combines molded keycaps, textured grip areas, and a soft-touch finish on key contact surfaces. These details are intended to improve control and comfort during regular use.

Finish, switch type, accessories, and packaging can be confirmed during the sample stage for wholesale and customization projects.
