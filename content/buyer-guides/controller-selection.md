The right controller is the one that matches your target platform, customer and retail positioning. These five models serve different briefs; use their product pages for the full configuration and confirm the selected version before listing or ordering.

## Start with your customer's use case

### G17 — a PS5-compatible starting point

Choose [G17](/products/g17/) if your range needs a PS5-compatible controller. Its current product page lists Hall Effect joysticks, four programmable back buttons and integrated audio controls. An unbranded evaluation sample can be requested, subject to the current configuration and availability. Confirm the functions needed on each target platform before committing to retail copy.

### G1 — broad features in a balanced range

[G1](/products/g1/) combines TMR joysticks, micro-switch ABXY buttons and triggers that switch between a longer Hall Effect travel and a short micro-switch action. This is a useful starting point when you want a feature-rich controller without positioning it as your most premium option. Compare the exact bundle and connection modes on the product page.

### G2 — premium differentiation

[G2](/products/g2/) adds TMR joysticks, a battery-percentage display and a switchable Xbox-style/NS button layout. Its triggers also switch between long Hall Effect travel and short micro-switch action. The charging dock is **optional**, not part of the standard package; confirm the bundle when quoting.

### G6 Pro — multi-platform value

[G6 Pro](/products/g6-pro/) focuses on broad platform coverage and core functions, with Hall Effect joysticks and Bluetooth, 2.4GHz and wired connection options. Its Switch 2 compatibility is for gameplay; the product page states it does not wake the console. Confirm the receiver and final bundle, as the receiver is optional.

### G7 — fast input and tactile face buttons

[G7](/products/g7/) is a fit for buyers looking for micro-switch ABXY buttons and a polling rate of up to 1000Hz in wired and 2.4GHz modes. Polling rate is mode-dependent; do not apply that figure to Bluetooth operation. Confirm the receiver and the final bundle before publishing a listing.
