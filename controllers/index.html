Choose controllers for your product range

Compare TMR wireless controllers such as the G1 with Hall Effect models such as the G9. Check each model’s connection modes, button layout and platform support before choosing a sample for your store or distribution range.

For a performance-focused range, the G7 tri-mode controller supports up to 1000Hz polling in wired and 2.4GHz modes. The G9 supports up to 1000Hz in 2.4GHz mode. Wireless receivers are optional accessories on these two models; confirm your required bundle when enquiring.

Confirm compatibility before ordering

Compatibility and features vary by model, platform, game and connection mode. For example, G7 and G9 support Switch 2 gameplay but do not support console wake-up. Use the individual product specifications to check the functions your customers need.
