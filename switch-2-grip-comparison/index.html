Which product fits your range?

GA05: a focused ergonomic accessory

Choose GA05 when your customers want additional palm support without a charging bridge. Classic White and Sakura Pink are listed on the product page. Small wholesale orders are welcome; ask for the current stock, available colors and order quantity.

GA06L: a charging grip with room for your brand

Choose GA06L for detached Joy-Con 2 play with USB-C charging input and three interchangeable back-button styles. Artwork can extend across the center bridge and back buttons. Waterslide decals or water transfer printing are evaluated against the design and approved sample.

GA06P: both products in one kit

Choose GA06P when your retail program needs the GA05 grip pair and GA06L charging grip together. Confirm the box contents, selected finish and packaging layout before ordering.

Frequently asked questions

Does GA05 charge the Joy-Con 2 controllers?

No. GA05 is a pair of individual ergonomic grips. Charging is provided by GA06L, which is also included in GA06P.

Does GA06L work while the Joy-Con 2 controllers are attached to the console?

The charging grip is intended for detached Joy-Con 2 controllers in tabletop or TV-mode play. It does not attach to the console as a handheld charging accessory.

What is included in GA06P?

The kit combines the charging grip, three interchangeable GL/GR back-button sets, a pair of individual grips and a USB-C cable. Confirm the final order specification; Joy-Con 2 controllers are not included.

Can the center bridge and back buttons have matching graphics?

GA06L supports coordinated graphic proposals across these surfaces, subject to design and sample approval.
